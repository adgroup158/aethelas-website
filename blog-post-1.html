Aethelas Launches Verifiable AI Memory Platform

September 6, 2026 5 min read Akash Deep

Today, we're proud to announce the launch of Aethelas — a trust layer for verifiable AI memory. After two years of research, engineering, and testing, we're making the platform publicly available to developers, enterprises, and researchers.

Aethelas solves a fundamental problem in AI: black-box memory. Modern AI systems can learn, but you can't prove what they learned, when they learned it, or how to remove specific data from their memory. In regulated industries — finance, healthcare, government — this opacity makes AI deployment nearly impossible.

💡 The core insight: Forgetting-resistance and unlearning are not separate mechanisms — they are the same replay operation run forward (include everything) or with an exclusion set (omit specific examples).

What We Built

Aethelas wraps any AI model with a cryptographic ledger that records every training event, every decision, and every memory operation. The platform provides four fundamental operations:

  • Learn — Train continuously as new data arrives, without full retraining.
  • Remember — Replay from permanent memory to resist catastrophic forgetting.
  • Prove — Show exactly what the model knew at any past point, cryptographically.
  • Unlearn — Remove specific data on request, with a proof the deletion occurred.

Production Status

The platform is live with real infrastructure:

  • 20 active nodes running on cloud infrastructure
  • 84/84 unit tests passing — every component verified
  • 18/18 red-team attacks blocked — including signature forgery, replay attacks, and injection attempts
  • ~5 second block times with deterministic BFT finality
  • 89.5% capability benchmark across seven evaluation suites

What Makes Aethelas Different

Most systems that combine AI and blockchain treat the two as separate layers. Aethelas embeds the AI decision process into transaction validation itself. Every transaction carries an AIDecision record produced by an on-node model, and validator voting power is weighted by an AI-derived reliability score — a consensus mechanism we call iPoS (intelligent Proof of Stake).

"A system whose value is trust reports its ceilings rather than hiding them."

Honest by Design

We believe in publishing our limitations alongside our strengths. Our benchmark suite reports a 70% continual-learning score — a genuine weakness in class-incremental learning. We report this because an evaluation that cannot fail cannot measure progress.
