🏥 Case Study 02

Provable Deletion for Medical Data

📅 Q1 2026 ⏱ 4 weeks deployment 🏢 Healthcare AI Platform 🔒 Aethelas Memory · Provable Deletion

Challenge

A healthcare AI platform handling sensitive patient data needed to comply with India's DPDP Act and the EU's GDPR. Patient consent could be revoked at any time, requiring immediate and verifiable deletion of all associated data from the system. The platform needed to prove to auditors that deletion had occurred — without trusting any single node.

Solution

Aethelas's decentralized storage layer (TR-12) with provable deletion was deployed. Patient data was erasure-coded across 5 nodes (k=3) and encrypted. When a deletion request was received, all fragments were purged and a cryptographic proof was generated.
